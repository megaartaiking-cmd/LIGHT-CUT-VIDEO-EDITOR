Built for older PCs

No install and no large download: LiteCut runs entirely in the browser, offers a low-load ¼ preview mode, and renders exports in small chunks to stay usable on older single-core machines. Actual minimums depend on your browser and operating system — Windows 10 itself needs 1 GB RAM (32-bit) / 2 GB (64-bit) — so treat 512 MB-class hardware as the design target, best paired with a lightweight browser and 480p exports.
